    V3 prototype limitation: smartphone IMU dead reckoning is not a precision vehicle-navigation solution. Accelerometer integration accumulates error quickly, and phone orientation varies with mounting. V3 therefore uses GNSS speed/heading when available to calibrate the motion model, applies conservative drift limits during GNSS loss, and always performs a position correction when GNSS returns. For a real vehicle, use a calibrated IMU, fixed mounting, wheel-speed/odometry and a proper EKF/UKF.
